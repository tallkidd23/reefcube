THE MODEL

From soil to swarm.

The cube is logically one million cells. Soil occupies the substrate, water is the unoccupied navigable field, and life is counted by the actual cube cells occupied by plants and organisms.

life / water = biological voxels ÷ available water voxels

A fish is not a rectangle placed inside the cube. It is a connected voxel organism moving through the cube.
